Lynx mark

Two drawings of one head. The full mark is for 24px and up. The 16px mark is redrawn on a pixel grid for favicons and anything smaller than 24px. Both come in colour and dark. The full mark keeps its tones on dark and only recolours the white around the eye; the 16px mark lifts every tone a step, since the deep tone does not read that small on a dark tab bar. The full mark also comes in mono (one colour, currentColor).
